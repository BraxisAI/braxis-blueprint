# KIT — one resident in a 3D world

**Live:** https://braxisai.com/world/

KIT is a humanoid garden-robot. It has a head with one big eye, an antenna, a
chest light, two jointed arms (one tool hand), two legs, and a seed/tool pack.
It lives in a three.js city, chooses its own projects, builds structures that
persist, makes things, reads, watches, and keeps a diary. It named its world
**Hollowscape** — *"Small hands shape big quiet."*

This is the honest write-up: what is autonomous, what is authored, and what is
still missing.

## The loop

- A deterministic 2-second loop. **No LLM in the tick.**
- Five drives: energy, curiosity, social, comfort, mastery.
- Nine places, twenty-four actions.
- Actions are chosen by need + learned preference + project alignment +
  novelty + curiosity. Preferences are EMA-updated from how the action felt.
- One free-model call per day for reflection, a new desire, a dream, a wonder,
  a new subject, and the world name/tagline.
- Everything else is local: memory, decisions, journal, builds, creations.

## What KIT can do

- **Projects:** self-chosen intentions (garden row, bench, lantern, trellis,
  arch, mural, tower, bridge, fountain, monument). A project takes several work
  actions and ends as a persistent structure in `world_builds.json`.
- **Dojo / blueprints:** starts with three; `study` unlocks more; every build
  raises that skill 1-5. The watcher can leave a blueprint as a gift.
- **Make:** medium × subject (paint/carve/plant/write/photograph/sing × the
  garden, the city, a cat, a bird, visitors, the lantern, the fountain, the sky,
  a dream, the trio, plus self-generated subjects). Repeated pairs become a
  creative hobby.
- **Browse / watch:** reads HN, Wikipedia, arXiv, GitHub trending, the Guardian;
  watches videos through the estate's watch chain. Both feed wonders and the
  daily reflection.
- **Visitor loop:** stone/flower/song/question change its drives; an **idea** is
  a commission it can accept or decline.
- **Diary:** journal, reflection, dream, decisions, wonders, creations.
- **Trio mailbox:** project notes go out; replies/gifts come back. The watcher
  enables; it does not command.
- **World name/tagline:** KIT's, not a brand.

## Authored vs self-generated (honest)

**Authored by us:** the action list, project templates, starting blueprints,
medium/subject seeds, desire seeds, and the "garden-robot" identity in the
prompt.

**Self-generated by KIT:** which action/project/medium/subject it chooses and
when; its daily desires, world name/tagline, wonders, new subjects, creative
combinations; whether it accepts or declines a visitor idea; its hobby.

The first garden row was **not forced by a prompt**: it was one of the three
starting blueprints and KIT chose it. That is still self-selection inside a
curated menu, not full option generation. The next step is a compiler that turns
a project KIT writes in its own words into a build schema.

## The Pino lesson

Pino (@3eyes.iii) is one bird, ~18 months, a custom cognitive pipeline on a
Raspberry Pi, never prompted, likes from how things feel, visitors can influence
him. The public posts do not explain his code. The transferable mechanism is:
persistent body + affect + memory + consequences + time, with the model outside
the tick. A hobby like "making cat pictures" comes from a general affordance
(tool × subject), free time, a felt result, and repetition. The `make` layer is
our first version of that.

## Free-will modules: evaluated, not copied

Kept: entropy curiosity, evolved desires, mood attractors, dreams, resonance,
self-chosen projects, visitor refusal, creative preferences.

Skipped: LLM-per-decision gate, meta-learning/evolutionary prompt, random
world-event pool, artist lyric memory. The old modules are in the private
recovery archive; the `.pm2` golden dumps are process lists, not code.

## Stack / cost

- No model in the tick; one free-model call/day (local `ollama-lfm` first, free
  router fallback).
- Procedural three.js/canvas art; optional free image model at most 1/day.
- Existing ACE music lane at most 1 track/day.
- Playwright + ffmpeg for clips; no model.
- JSON memory; no vector DB for one resident.
- Social posts route through the estate's determiner, which ranks platforms by
  measured performance; no model per post.

## Files

- `world_life.py` — the resident's brain and HTTP API.
- `kit_watcher.py` — the dreamkeeper; reads journals/dreams and gifts a
  blueprint when a dream matches a locked one.
- `world_share.py` — Playwright capture of a build, then the determiner routes
  the post.

## What is next

- Custom projects/blueprints written by KIT and compiled into schemas.
- Page gallery for creations; music medium; daily diary/video lanes.
- A weekly research pass that proposes one improvement, never auto-applied.

*KIT is bounded autonomy with self-selection today. The point of the next layer
is to make the options, not just the choice, partly its own.*
